04 / Better comparison notes

What belongs in a useful product spreadsheet?

Use consistent fields so a lower displayed price does not hide a different size, quantity or delivery charge.

Research fields to complete before ordering
FieldWhat to recordWhat it helps resolve
Exact optionSource URL, seller, color, size and quantityWhether two quotes cover the same item
FitGarment or footwear measurements, units and measuring methodWhether the selected size fits your reference measurements
EvidenceListing images, any order-specific QC photos and unanswered questionsWhich details are visible and which still need confirmation
Total costOriginal currency, conversion date, delivery and confirmed feesWhether the full purchase fits your budget
Purchase conditionsStock status, return eligibility, deadlines and quoted feesWhat options remain if the item is unsuitable

A photograph can help you inspect visible details. It does not establish authenticity, composition, durability or seller reliability.

05 / Useful answers

PikoBuy spreadsheet FAQ

What is a PikoBuy spreadsheet?

It is an organized product research list used by people exploring shopping through PikoBuy. A useful list keeps product links, options, measurements, prices and research notes together. This page provides an independent guide and a CAFANS product snapshot rather than an official PikoBuy inventory.

Where do the buttons take me?

The “PikoBuy spreadsheet” and “Buy it now” buttons both take you to https://cafans.com. They do not place an order or open a PikoBuy checkout. Review the destination listing and available agent options before purchasing.

Are these real CAFANS products?

The six names, images and estimated USD prices were taken from the CAFANS homepage when this page was prepared. They are source listings, not independently tested recommendations. Recheck availability and the selected variation on CAFANS.

Are the prices final delivered prices?

No. The displayed figures are CAFANS product-price estimates. Domestic delivery, international shipping, service charges, payment fees and applicable taxes may add to your total. Use current quotes rather than treating missing charges as zero.

Does every product have QC photos?

This page does not verify order-specific QC availability. The images shown are catalog listing images. Ask the purchasing service whether inspection photographs are provided for your actual order and check the available review window.

How do I download and use the spreadsheet?

Select Shortlist on the products you want, then choose Download shortlist CSV. Open the file in Excel, Google Sheets or another spreadsheet application. It contains the catalog names, estimated prices, source homepage and blank fields for exact listing links, sizes and notes. With no items selected, the export includes all six products.

Is my shortlist stored?

The shortlist is held only in this page’s current session and resets when you reload. Download the CSV to keep your research. This page does not create an account or send your search terms or calculator entries to a server.
